# NotRealOrg — Security Policy

## 1. Is multi-factor authentication mandatory?
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for company accounts that support it.

## 2. What should a company password contain?
Passwords should be unique, difficult to guess, and compliant with the identity system's password requirements. Employees must not reuse company passwords on personal services.

## 3. Can employees share their passwords?
No. Passwords and authentication codes must never be shared with other people.

## 4. What should I do if I receive a suspicious email?
Do not click links or open unexpected attachments. Report the message using the company's phishing-reporting mechanism.

## 5. What should I do if I clicked a suspicious link?
Immediately report the incident to the Security Team and follow their instructions. Do not attempt to hide or delete evidence.

## 6. Can company files be stored on personal cloud drives?
No. Company confidential information must be stored only in approved company-managed systems.

## 7. Can employees use personal USB drives?
Personal USB storage devices must not be used to transfer company data unless specifically approved by Security.

## 8. How should confidential documents be shared?
Use approved company storage and collaboration tools with access limited to people who need the information.

## 9. What is considered a security incident?
Examples include lost devices, suspected account compromise, accidental data disclosure, phishing, malware, and unauthorized access.

## 10. How quickly should security incidents be reported?
Security incidents should be reported immediately after discovery.

## 11. Can employees use public Wi-Fi for work?
Public Wi-Fi should be avoided for sensitive work. When unavoidable, employees should use the approved company VPN and follow security guidance.

## 12. What should I do if I suspect my account has been compromised?
Change the password if possible, revoke suspicious sessions where supported, and immediately contact the Security Team.

## 13. Can confidential information be sent to a personal email account?
No. Confidential company information must not be forwarded to personal email accounts.

## 14. Are security training courses mandatory?
Assigned security-awareness training is mandatory and must be completed by the stated deadline.
