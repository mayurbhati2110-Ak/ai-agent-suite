# NotRealOrg — Benefits and Payroll FAQ

## 1. When is salary paid?
Salary is normally paid on the last working day of each month.

## 2. How can employees access their payslips?
Payslips are available through the employee portal after payroll processing is completed.

## 3. What should an employee do if their salary is incorrect?
The employee should contact Payroll Operations and provide the relevant payslip and supporting details.

## 4. Does NotRealOrg provide health insurance?
Eligible full-time employees are enrolled in the company's standard health-insurance program according to the applicable plan terms.

## 5. Can dependents be added to health insurance?
Eligible employees may request dependent coverage during the applicable enrollment period. Additional premiums may apply.

## 6. When can employees change benefits selections?
Benefits changes are normally permitted during the annual enrollment window or after qualifying life events.

## 7. Does NotRealOrg provide a retirement benefit?
Eligible employees may participate in the company's retirement savings program according to the plan rules.

## 8. Who handles payroll questions?
Payroll Operations handles salary, payslip, tax-document, and payroll-correction questions.

## 9. Are bonuses guaranteed?
No. Bonuses are not guaranteed unless a specific written employment agreement states otherwise.

## 10. Does NotRealOrg offer an employee referral bonus?
An employee referral bonus may be available for selected roles listed in the referral program. The program terms determine eligibility and payment.

## 11. How are tax documents provided?
Required tax documents are made available through the employee portal or the payroll process.

## 12. Does the company provide meal vouchers?
The current knowledge base does not define a meal-voucher benefit. Employees should contact People Operations for confirmation.
