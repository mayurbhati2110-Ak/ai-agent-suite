# NotRealOrg — Customer Support FAQ

## 1. What are customer support hours?
Standard customer support hours are Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

## 2. What is the standard response time?
Standard customer support requests receive an initial response within one business day.

## 3. How are critical issues handled?
Critical production issues are prioritized and escalated to the appropriate technical team.

## 4. What information should a customer provide when reporting an issue?
Customers should provide their account identifier, a description of the problem, steps to reproduce it, relevant timestamps, and screenshots or error messages when available.

## 5. How can a customer request a refund?
Refund requests should be submitted through the customer support channel with the order or invoice reference.

## 6. How are billing questions handled?
Billing questions are routed to the Billing Support team for review.

## 7. Can customers request a product feature?
Yes. Customers can submit feature requests through customer support. Requests are reviewed but are not guaranteed to be implemented.

## 8. What happens when support cannot resolve an issue?
Support escalates the case to the relevant specialist team and keeps the customer informed according to the case priority.

## 9. How should a customer report a security concern?
Security concerns should be reported through the security contact channel rather than ordinary product support.

## 10. Can support agents change customer account ownership?
Account ownership changes require verification and follow the account-management procedure.

## 11. Does customer support operate on public holidays?
Support availability may differ on company-designated holidays. Customers should check the published support schedule for the relevant date.

## 12. What information should customers avoid sending in support tickets?
Customers should not send passwords, authentication codes, payment-card numbers, or other unnecessary sensitive credentials in support tickets.
