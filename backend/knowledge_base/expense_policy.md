# NotRealOrg — Expense and Travel Policy

## 1. Which expenses can be reimbursed?
Reasonable business expenses incurred for approved company activities may be reimbursed when they comply with the expense policy.

## 2. How long do employees have to submit an expense claim?
Expense claims should normally be submitted within 30 days of the expense date.

## 3. What documentation is required for expenses?
Employees should provide an itemized receipt and the business purpose. Additional documentation may be required for travel expenses.

## 4. Are business travel expenses reimbursed?
Approved business travel expenses may be reimbursed according to the travel policy and applicable limits.

## 5. Who approves business travel?
Business travel requires manager approval before booking, unless an emergency exception applies.

## 6. What is the meal reimbursement limit?
The standard meal reimbursement limit is 25 currency units per person per meal for domestic business travel.

## 7. Can an employee claim a meal without a receipt?
A missing receipt should be explained in the expense system and may require manager approval. Reimbursement is not guaranteed.

## 8. Can employees choose business-class flights?
Economy class is the standard for flights under six hours. Business class requires an approved exception.

## 9. Are taxis reimbursed?
Reasonable taxi or ride-hailing costs for approved business travel may be reimbursed when documented.

## 10. Are personal expenses reimbursable?
Personal expenses are not reimbursable unless the policy explicitly identifies them as an approved benefit.

## 11. What happens if an expense claim is rejected?
The employee can review the rejection reason, correct missing information where applicable, and resubmit the claim.

## 12. Who handles expense-policy questions?
Finance Operations handles questions about reimbursements and expense claims.
