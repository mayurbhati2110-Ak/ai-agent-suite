# NotRealOrg — Leave and Attendance Policy

## 1. How many annual leave days are provided?
Full-time employees receive 18 paid annual leave days per calendar year.

## 2. How many sick leave days are provided?
Full-time employees receive 10 paid sick leave days per calendar year.

## 3. How many casual leave days are provided?
Employees receive 6 paid casual leave days per calendar year.

## 4. Can unused annual leave be carried forward?
Up to 5 unused annual leave days may be carried into the following calendar year. Additional unused days expire unless an exception is approved.

## 5. Can sick leave be carried forward?
Unused sick leave does not normally carry forward to the next calendar year.

## 6. How far in advance should planned leave be requested?
Planned leave should normally be requested at least three working days in advance.

## 7. What should an employee do in an emergency absence?
The employee should inform their manager as soon as reasonably possible and submit the leave request afterward.

## 8. Who approves leave?
The employee's direct manager approves leave requests, subject to staffing and policy requirements.

## 9. Can leave be cancelled after approval?
An employee may ask their manager to cancel an approved leave request. Cancellation depends on operational requirements and the manager's approval.

## 10. How is attendance recorded?
Attendance is recorded through the employee attendance portal. Employees are responsible for correcting missing or incorrect entries promptly.

## 11. What happens if an employee forgets to record attendance?
The employee should submit a correction request through the attendance portal and notify their manager if required.

## 12. Are public holidays counted as annual leave?
Company-designated public holidays are not deducted from an employee's annual leave balance.
