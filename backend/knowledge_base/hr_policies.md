# NotRealOrg — HR Policies

## 1. Who handles HR requests?
People Operations handles employee records, policy questions, onboarding, and general employment matters.

## 2. How should an employee update personal information?
Employees should submit a personal-information update through the employee portal and notify People Operations if supporting documents are required.

## 3. How are performance reviews conducted?
Performance reviews consider goals, role expectations, collaboration, quality of work, and manager feedback.

## 4. Can employees request a change in reporting manager?
Employees may raise concerns about reporting relationships with People Operations. Changes are reviewed individually and are not automatic.

## 5. What is the internal transfer process?
An employee interested in an internal transfer should discuss the opportunity with their manager and apply through the internal jobs portal.

## 6. Can employees work for another company while employed by NotRealOrg?
Outside employment that creates a conflict of interest or interferes with an employee's duties is not permitted without written approval from People Operations.

## 7. What is the policy for workplace conduct?
Employees are expected to behave professionally, respectfully, and without harassment or discrimination.

## 8. How should workplace misconduct be reported?
Concerns about misconduct can be reported to People Operations or through the confidential reporting channel.

## 9. Can an employee request a reference letter?
Employees may request an employment verification or reference according to the company's verification process. Requests should be submitted to People Operations.

## 10. What happens when an employee leaves the company?
Departing employees must complete the offboarding checklist, return company property, transfer required work, and complete applicable exit procedures.
