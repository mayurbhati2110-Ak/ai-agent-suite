# NotRealOrg — IT Support FAQ

## 1. How do I reset my company password?
Use the password-reset option on the NotRealOrg identity portal. If the reset fails, contact IT Support.

## 2. What should I do if my company laptop stops working?
Restart the device if possible and contact IT Support through the service desk. Do not attempt hardware repairs yourself.

## 3. How do I request software installation?
Submit a software-installation request through the IT service desk. Software may require security and licensing approval.

## 4. How do I request VPN access?
VPN access must be requested through the IT service desk. Access is granted after the required approval.

## 5. What should I do if the VPN is not connecting?
Check your internet connection, restart the VPN client, and retry. If the problem continues, open an IT support ticket with the error message.

## 6. How do I report a lost company laptop?
Immediately contact IT Support and the Security Team. The device should be reported as lost as soon as possible.

## 7. How do I request a replacement laptop?
Open a hardware request with IT Support and provide the reason for replacement. IT will evaluate the request.

## 8. What should I do if my company email is not working?
Check the service status page and restart your mail application. If the problem persists, contact IT Support.

## 9. Can I install any application on a company laptop?
No. Employees should install only approved software. Unapproved applications may be blocked or removed for security reasons.

## 10. How do I connect to a company printer?
Use the printer setup instructions in the IT portal or contact IT Support if the required printer is not listed.

## 11. What is the IT support response target?
Standard IT requests have an initial response target of one business day. Critical incidents are prioritized separately.

## 12. Can IT remotely access my laptop?
IT may use approved remote-support tools when necessary to diagnose or resolve an issue, subject to company security procedures.
