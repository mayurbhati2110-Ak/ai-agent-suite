# NotRealOrg — Remote Work Policy

## 1. Who is eligible for remote work?
Remote work eligibility depends on the employee's role, team requirements, and manager approval.

## 2. How many remote days are normally allowed?
Eligible employees may work remotely up to two days per week unless their team has a different approved arrangement.

## 3. Can an employee work remotely from another city?
Remote work from another city requires manager approval and must comply with company and legal requirements.

## 4. Can an employee work remotely from another country?
International remote work is not automatically permitted. Employees must obtain written approval from People Operations and meet applicable legal, tax, and security requirements.

## 5. What equipment is provided for remote work?
Eligible employees may receive a company laptop and standard work accessories required for their role.

## 6. Is home internet reimbursed?
A standard home-internet reimbursement of up to 30 currency units per month is available to eligible remote employees, subject to the expense process.

## 7. What happens if internet connectivity fails during remote work?
Employees should notify their manager, use an approved alternative connection when practical, and follow their team's continuity procedures.

## 8. Are remote employees expected to attend meetings?
Yes. Remote employees are expected to attend required meetings and remain reachable during their agreed working hours.

## 9. Can a manager require an employee to work from the office?
Yes. Business, collaboration, training, or operational needs may require an eligible remote employee to attend the office.

## 10. Can employees work remotely during probation?
Remote work during probation is subject to manager approval and team requirements.
