# NotRealOrg — Company Overview

## 1. What is NotRealOrg?
NotRealOrg is a fictional technology company used for demonstration and testing of the AI Knowledge-Base Support Agent.

## 2. What does NotRealOrg do?
NotRealOrg develops software products and provides technology services for business customers.

## 3. What is the company's working week?
The standard working week is Monday through Friday.

## 4. What are standard working hours?
Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday through Friday, with a one-hour lunch break.

## 5. Where is NotRealOrg headquartered?
The fictional headquarters is listed as 100 Innovation Avenue, New Harbor.

## 6. What is the company mission?
NotRealOrg's mission is to build dependable technology products that make business operations simpler.

## 7. What is the company communication language?
English is the standard language for official company communication and documentation.

## 8. Who can answer general company questions?
Employees should contact People Operations for general company-policy questions.

## 9. What is the probation period?
New full-time employees have a six-month probation period unless their employment agreement states otherwise.

## 10. How often are performance reviews conducted?
Formal performance reviews are conducted twice a year. Managers may also provide informal feedback throughout the year.
